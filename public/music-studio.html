History, sources and simulation limits

Era thresholds are a game-design timeline, not a claim that every society developed simultaneously. Military formations, treasury, population, connections and production are abstract game values. History is not forced to repeat after the opening.

Routes represent regional access, not shared national borders. Sea routes require navigation technology; ocean routes unlock later. Terrain, supply, morale and fortifications influence battles. Each center can mobilize once per turn.

Research unlocks period-appropriate units, better production and sea connections. Trade and stable civilian development build prosperity. There are no ideological bonuses, nuclear attacks or scripted atrocities.
